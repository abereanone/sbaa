]]> By: John Taylor https://southernbaptistsabolishingabortion.com/2021/09/29/incremental-regulationism-mans-wisdom-or-true-wisdom/comment-page-1/#comment-62 Thu, 04 Nov 2021 18:45:00 +0000 http://southernbaptistsabolishingabortion.com/?p=362#comment-62 I believe the Roe v. Wade emperor has no clothes, and one essential Abolitionist strategy is to expose its unconstitutional nakedness. Roe is NOT “the law of the land”, NOT a legal straitjacket hindering abortion’s abolition. Its Achilles heel is its unconstitutionality, a brazen theft of power by the Supreme Court that “the supreme law of the land” delegated to the States.

a) The Constitution nowhere delegates to the Federal Government any powers pertaining to commercial services, including abortion; those are State matters. Instead, the Constitution grants to the Federal Government enumerated, powers (Art. 1, Sec. 8; 10th Amendment) that can be counted, like the cars on freight train. A train with 23 cars does not magically consist of 24, or 35, or 46 just because someone says to. The Federal Government keeps adding cars to the freight train, and Roe v. Wade is by far the worst.

b) Even if the Constitution did include abortion by an imaginative expansion of the Federal Government’s limited powers, it is Congress, not the Supreme Court, that should exercise that power by legislation. In the very first words that follow the Preamble:

ALL legislative Powers herein granted shall be vested in a Congress of the United States, which shall consist of a Senate and House of Representatives. (emphasis added)

President Lincoln warned of precisely this sort of abuse of Supreme Court power in his First Inaugural Address delivered March 4, 1861, barely a month before the outbreak of war. We must recall it was barely four years after the notorious Dred Scott decision of 1857 when the President said:

“[T]he candid citizen must confess that if the policy of the Government upon vital questions affecting the whole people is to be irrevocably fixed by decisions of the Supreme Court, the instant they are made in ordinary litigation between parties in personal actions the people will have ceased to be their own rulers, having to that extent practically resigned their Government into the hands of that eminent tribunal.”

Roe v. Wade is thus a brazen power grab. It is high time the States, in the President’s words, “be their own rulers” again, reclaim their stolen power, and criminalize all abortions, boldly indifferent to any judicial “review” that cites Roe v. Wade. All they lack is better knowledge of our traditions as they were originally intended and their history – and gumption.
